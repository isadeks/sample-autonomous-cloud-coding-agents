## Notes

- Regenerate this inventory rather than hand-editing it; it is derived from file
  headers and `export` statements.
- The `orchestration-*` family under `shared/` is the largest cluster: plan
  decomposition, DAG storage, reconciliation, rollup, and per-channel comment
  rendering (Slack / Linear / Jira).
- The `*-webhook.ts` / `*-webhook-processor.ts` pairs follow a consistent split:
  the webhook Lambda verifies the signature and enqueues, the processor Lambda
  does the work asynchronously.
